# Smart-Study-Planner
Cale Dietzel
The Smart Study Planner is a web-based application designed to help students organize their academic responsibilities and manage their time more effectively. The system will allow students to create and manage assignments, exams, projects, and study tasks while considering deadlines, task difficulty, priority, and available study time. The application will generate personalized study schedules to help students determine what they should work on and when, while also providing features such as progress tracking, calendar views, reminders, and productivity analytics. The primary users of the Smart Study Planner are high school and college students who need a simple and organized way to balance multiple academic responsibilities. By combining task management with automated scheduling, the application aims to reduce stress, improve organization, and help students stay on track with their academic goals.
